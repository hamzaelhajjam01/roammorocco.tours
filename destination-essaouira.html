The Bohemian Coastal Soul of Morocco

Formerly known by its Portuguese name Mogador, Essaouira is Morocco’s most charming coastal haven. Unlike the bustling labyrinth of Marrakech, Essaouira’s whitewashed medina with its bright blue shutters was laid out by French architect Théodore Cornut in 1760 with wide, breezy avenues designed to capture the ocean air.

It has long enchanted artists, musicians, and travelers—from Orson Welles (who filmed Othello along its ramparts) to Jimi Hendrix. With Roam Morocco, you leave Marrakech behind at 08:30 AM in executive comfort, escaping city heat for the refreshing maritime breeze.

Your private chauffeur greets you at your riad at 08:30 AM. Travel west through the semi-arid plains into the UNESCO-protected Argan Biosphere Reserve. Frequently Asked Questions: Essaouira Tours

Can we stay overnight in Essaouira?

Yes, absolutely! Spending 1 or 2 nights in a boutique coastal riad or luxury oceanfront villa is one of the most relaxing additions to any Morocco itinerary. We can tailor this tour as a one-way transfer or multi-day coastal getaway.

Is Essaouira easy to walk around without a guide?

Yes! Unlike Fes or Marrakech, Essaouira’s medina is compact and straightforward with wide rectilinear streets. It is virtually impossible to get lost, making self-guided exploration very pleasant. However, a private licensed guide can be arranged upon request.
