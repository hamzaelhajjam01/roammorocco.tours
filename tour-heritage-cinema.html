Heritage & Cinema: The Hollywood of Africa

Embark on the ultimate journey through Morocco's cinematic history and ancient caravan routes. The Heritage & Cinema Tour takes you across the spectacular High Atlas Mountains to explore legendary Kasbahs, including the famous Ait Benhaddou, and the Ouarzazate film studios—the true Hollywood of Africa.

Why Take an Ait Benhaddou Tour?

If you are researching the best Morocco day trips or multi-day excursions, an Ait Benhaddou tour is absolutely essential. Designated as a UNESCO World Heritage site since 1987, the Ksar of Ait Benhaddou is a striking example of southern Moroccan architecture. Wandering through its earthen clay walls is like stepping back in time to when the trans-Saharan trade routes flourished.

Beyond its historical significance, it is a mecca for film buffs. If you've ever wanted to visit the Game of Thrones Morocco filming locations (Yunkai) or see where Russell Crowe fought in Gladiator, this Ouarzazate tour brings those cinematic fantasies to life.

Tour Highlights

  • ✦ Tizi n'Tichka Pass: Cross the High Atlas Mountains at a breathtaking 2,260m elevation.
  • ✦ Ait Benhaddou: Enjoy a private, guided walking tour of the iconic UNESCO Kasbah.
  • ✦ Atlas Film Studios: Go behind the scenes at the massive Ouarzazate film studios.
  • ✦ Kasbah Taourirt: Discover the beautifully restored 19th-century palace in the heart of Ouarzazate.

Detailed Itinerary

The Journey Across the High Atlas

We depart early in a luxury 4x4 or private van, ascending the winding roads of the High Atlas Mountains. We'll stop frequently at panoramic viewpoints overlooking lush Berber villages and deep valleys before conquering the famous Tizi n'Tichka pass.

Exploring Kasbah Ait Benhaddou

Arriving at the edge of the Ounila River, you'll cross over to the ancient fortified village. Your expert local guide will lead you through the labyrinthine alleys of Ait Benhaddou, explaining the traditional mud-brick building techniques and the rich history of the Glaoui family. Don't forget your camera—every corner looks like a movie set.

Ouarzazate: The Hollywood of Africa

After a traditional Moroccan tagine lunch, we drive a short distance to Ouarzazate. Here, you get exclusive access to the Atlas Film Studios. Walk past towering Egyptian statues and ancient Roman temples abandoned in the desert after filming wrapped. We conclude the day with a visit to the intricately decorated Kasbah Taourirt before driving back.

FAQ: Ouarzazate & Ait Benhaddou

What movies were filmed at Ait Benhaddou?

A staggering number of blockbuster films and TV shows have used the Kasbah as a backdrop. Some of the most famous include Gladiator, Game of Thrones (as the city of Yunkai), The Mummy, Lawrence of Arabia, and Kingdom of Heaven.

How far is Ouarzazate from Marrakech?

The drive from Marrakech to Ouarzazate takes approximately 4 hours, crossing the High Atlas Mountains. While the distance is only about 190 kilometers (118 miles), the winding mountain roads dictate a slower, scenic pace.

Can you visit the Atlas Film Studios?

Yes! The Atlas Film Studios in Ouarzazate are open to the public. Our tour includes guided access where you can walk through massive, intact movie sets from famous historical epics.
