The Cinematic Gateway to the Sahara

Rising like a monumental sandcastle from the banks of the Ounila river, the Ksar of Aït Benhaddou is arguably the most dramatic architectural wonder in Morocco. Built along the trans-Saharan caravan route that carried gold, ivory, and salt from Timbuktu to Marrakech, its red-clay watchtowers and fortified granaries have stood for nearly a thousand years.

Because of its pristine preservation, Hollywood directors fell in love with it decades ago. When you cross the river stones into its labyrinthine alleys with our local guide, you walk the exact paths where Russell Crowe fought in Gladiator and Daenerys Targaryen liberated Yunkai in Game of Thrones.
