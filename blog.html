Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best time to visit Morocco?

+
Spring (mid-March to May) and autumn (September to October) are ideal, offering pleasant warm weather ideal for medinas and the Sahara.

Do I need a visa to visit Morocco?

+
Citizens of the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and EU countries do not require a visa for stays up to 90 days. Always verify passport validity.

Is Morocco safe for tourists?

+
Yes, Morocco is considered very safe for international travelers. The local government prioritizes tourism safety and hospitality.

What should female travelers wear in Morocco?

+
Dressing modestly (covering shoulders and knees) is recommended out of respect for local customs, especially in historical medinas and rural villages.

Can you drink alcohol in Morocco?

+
Alcohol is available in licensed hotels, boutique riads, bars, and tourist restaurants, though drinking in public streets is prohibited.

What is the currency in Morocco?

+
The Moroccan Dirham (MAD). While major hotels accept credit cards, carrying cash is recommended for souk purchases and small cafes.
