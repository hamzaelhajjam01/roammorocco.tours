Escape the Red City into Pure Mountain Majesty

Just 90 minutes south of Marrakech’s bustling Medina lies an entirely different world. The High Atlas Mountains rise like an impenetrable granite wall, sheltering ancient Berber (Amazigh) communities whose hospitality is legendary across North Africa.

Our private excursion to Imlil Valley offers the perfect contrast to city life. You leave the city behind in an executive 4x4, ascending through the lush green valleys of Asni. In Imlil, you are welcomed by your dedicated native mountain guide—someone whose family has lived in these peaks for generations.

"Sitting on a simple Berber rooftop, sipping hot mint tea while listening to the rushing meltwater from Mount Toubkal, is when the true peace of Morocco sinks into your soul."

— ROAM MOROCCO MOUNTAIN LOG

You can walk along ancient irrigation channels through ancient walnut trees, visit the high village of Armed, or simply unwind and enjoy a feast of freshly baked bread, cold-pressed olive oil, and organic mountain tagines.

Frequently Asked Questions: High Atlas Tours

Can we do this tour if someone in our party has limited mobility?

Yes! We can arrange a gentle mule ride with an experienced handler so guests of all ages (or small children) can enjoy the scenic trails without physical strain. Alternatively, we can drive directly to panoramic mountain lodges like Kasbah Toubkal where you can relax on comfortable terrace lounges.

Does it snow in the Atlas Mountains?

Yes. The higher peaks around Mount Toubkal are covered in snow from November through April. The roads to Imlil are kept open and accessible year-round, and the snow-capped views from the valley during winter and spring are truly spectacular.
