## 1. 🌐 How to Submit to Microsoft Edge Add-ons (FREE — DA 98)

1. **Sign in to Microsoft Partner Center:**
   * Go to: [https://partner.microsoft.com/dashboard/microsoftedge](https://partner.microsoft.com/dashboard/microsoftedge)
   * Sign in with your Microsoft account (the same one used for Bing Webmaster Tools).
2. **Create New Extension:**
   * Click **"Create new extension"**.
   * Drag and drop the zip file:
     `c:\Users\hp\Downloads\roammorocco.tours\extensions\roammorocco-travel-companion-v1.0.0.zip`
3. **Fill in Store Listing Details (Where you get the Backlink!):**
   * **Name:** `Roam Morocco — Dirham (MAD) & Travel Companion`
   * **Short Description:** `Instant Moroccan Dirham (MAD) currency converter, local tipping calculator, and expert travel guide by Roam Morocco Tours.`
   * **Category:** Travel
   * **Website URL:** `https://roammorocco.tours/` *(This is your DA 98 backlink!)*
   * **Support Contact URL:** `https://roammorocco.tours/`
   * **Privacy Policy URL:** `https://roammorocco.tours/privacy-policy.html`
4. **Submit for Review:**
   * Click **Submit**. Microsoft Edge reviews lightweight utilities very quickly (typically 24–48 hours). Once published, your listing on `microsoftedge.microsoft.com` goes live with your backlink.

## 2. 🦊 How to Submit to Mozilla Firefox Add-ons (FREE — DA 98)

1. **Sign in to Firefox Developer Hub:**
   * Go to: [https://addons.mozilla.org/developers/addon/submit/upload-listed](https://addons.mozilla.org/developers/addon/submit/upload-listed)
   * Log in or create a free Firefox account.
2. **Upload Extension:**
   * Choose **"On this site"** (so it gets a public listing on AMO).
   * Upload `roammorocco-travel-companion-v1.0.0.zip`.
3. **Fill in Listing Details:**
   * **Homepage:** `https://roammorocco.tours/` *(Your DA 98 backlink!)*
   * **Support Website:** `https://roammorocco.tours/`
   * **Summary:** `Instant Moroccan Dirham (MAD) currency converter, local tipping calculator, and expert travel guide by Roam Morocco Tours.`
4. **Submit for Review:**
   * Automated verification runs instantly; human approval typically completes within 1–2 days.
