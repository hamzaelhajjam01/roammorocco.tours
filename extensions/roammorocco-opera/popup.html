ETIQUETTE Medina Bargaining Rules
  • Offer 50% First: In medina souks, vendors usually quote 40–50% above fair value. Offer half and meet in the middle.
  • Keep Small Cash: Vendors often don't have change for 200 MAD notes. Carry 20, 50, and 100 MAD bills.
  • Walk Away Politely: If a price feels too high, smile, say "Shukran" (thank you), and walk away. They will often call you back with the real price!
