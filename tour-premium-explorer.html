3-Day Detailed Itinerary

DAY 01 // 07:30 AM DEPARTURE

The Atlantic Coastal Escape to Essaouira

Depart Marrakech early in a private executive van. Travel through rolling argan forests, stopping at an organic women's cooperative to discover how liquid gold argan oil is cold-pressed. Arriving in breezy Essaouira (Mogador), wander the UNESCO-listed blue and white medina, explore the Skala du Port ramparts where Orson Welles filmed Othello, and enjoy lunch at a waterfront seafood stall before returning in the evening.

Field Note // Chichaoua Plain

“Near Chichaoua, the big tour buses pull into concrete gift barns with diesel exhaust in the air. We pull off the highway into a cooperative where Fatima and her sister still crack argan nuts between two river stones by hand. When you taste fresh culinary argan oil scooped with warm khobz—nutty, toasted, unrefined—you immediately understand how much of Morocco gets sanitized for mass travel.”

— Brahim, Roam Morocco Route Specialist

DAY 02 // 08:30 AM DEPARTURE

Ourika Valley, Setti Fatma Falls & Berber Heritage

Journey south into the lush foothills of the High Atlas Mountains. Trek along shaded mountain paths up to the seven cascades of Setti Fatma with an expert local mountain guide. Afterward, cross wooden footbridges to enjoy a traditional Moroccan feast served right at tables set into the shallow cooling mountain stream. Return to Marrakech with late afternoon free for riad spa treatments.

Field Note // Setti Fatma Stream

“The real tagine in the valley isn’t on the roadside terraces with laminated English menus. It’s tucked under walnut trees where Rachid simmers mountain lamb over wild olive embers in an unglazed clay dish seasoned by twenty seasons of cumin and wild thyme. You tear the crust of warm bread with your fingers while snowmelt from Toubkal rushes across the river stones below.”

— Brahim, Roam Morocco Route Specialist

DAY 03 // 03:30 PM DEPARTURE

The Secluded Agafay Desert Sunset & Laser Stargazing

Sleep in and spend your morning exploring the Yves Saint Laurent Museum and Majorelle Gardens. In the mid-afternoon, your driver whisks you into the serene stone dunes of the Agafay Desert (just 45 minutes from town). Embark on a peaceful golden-hour camel trek, dine under luxury nomadic tents, and finish the night with a guided astronomical laser presentation tracing Jupiter, Saturn, and desert constellations.

Field Note // Agafay Stone Dunes at Dusk

“People call Agafay a desert, but it is a plateau of bleached stone sculpted by wind. Once the day-tripper quad bikes roar back to Marrakech at five, an absolute silence falls across the plains like heavy linen. The High Atlas turns deep violet in the rear mirror, and the only sound is the desert breeze through wild scrub.”

— Lahcen, Roam Morocco Desert Specialist

Frequently Asked Questions: 3-Day Sahara Luxury Explorer

What luxury amenities are included in the Erg Chebbi desert camp?

Our handpicked luxury desert camps feature spacious private canvas pavilions with plush King or Queen beds, handcrafted Moroccan carpets, private en-suite bathrooms with hot-water showers, flushing porcelain toilets, electricity, bedside USB charging, and heating or AC. You also enjoy a candlelit 4-course Moroccan dinner and live campfire Gnawa drum performances under the stars.

How long is the camel trek to reach the luxury camp?

The sunset camel trek into the Erg Chebbi dunes takes approximately 45 to 60 minutes, timed perfectly for golden hour photography across the sand sea. For travelers who prefer not to ride camels or have mobility concerns, a private 4x4 dune transfer directly to your luxury tent entrance is always included at no extra charge.

What is the driving time between Marrakech and the Sahara Desert?

The journey covers approximately 560 km each way through the High Atlas, Dades Valley, and Todra Gorge. To ensure comfortable travel without rushing, our 3-Day Sahara Luxury Explorer breaks the drive with an overnight stay at a boutique kasbah hotel in Dades on Night 1, arriving at the Erg Chebbi dunes in the afternoon on Day 2.

Can this Sahara tour end in Fes instead of returning to Marrakech?

Yes! Many travelers book our 3-Day or 4-Day Desert route starting in Marrakech and finishing in Fes (or vice versa). On the final day, your private driver chauffeurs you north across the Ziz Valley gorge, the Middle Atlas cedar forest of Azrou, and the Alpine town of Ifrane, arriving in Fes by early evening.

Can dietary requirements (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, halal) be accommodated?

Yes, all meals (both at kasbah stops and at the desert camp) are freshly prepared and can easily accommodate vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, and nut-allergy requirements. Simply inform us when booking and your private hosts will curate tailored menus.
