FAQ: Travelling to Marrakech

Where is Marrakech located?

A common query is "where is Marrakech city?" It is situated in the western part of Morocco, just north of the foothills of the snow-capped High Atlas Mountains. It is a few hours inland from the coastal city of Casablanca.

Marrakesh or Marrakech: Which is correct?

Both are correct! "Marrakech" is the French spelling and is most commonly used in Morocco, while "Marrakesh" is the traditional English spelling.

What are the must-see places in Marrakech?

If you're looking for Marrakech top attractions within the city walls, do not miss the Jardin Majorelle, the Bahia Palace, the Saadian Tombs, and the world-famous Jemaa el-Fnaa square in the Marrakech old town.

Is Marrakech safe for tourists?

Yes, Marrakech tourism is heavily protected. Violent crime is extremely rare. As with any major tourist destination, visitors should be aware of petty theft (pickpocketing) in crowded areas like the souks and politely but firmly decline unsolicited "guides" offering directions.
