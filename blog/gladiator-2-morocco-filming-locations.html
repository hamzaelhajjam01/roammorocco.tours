Gladiator II Filming Locations in Morocco: Ait Benhaddou & Atlas Studios

Step behind the camera of Ridley Scott’s cinematic masterpiece. How to visit the UNESCO earthen fortress of Ait Benhaddou, Ouarzazate’s legendary film studios, and the desert landscapes of Hollywood in Morocco.

Published: October 3, 2026 Author: Hamza & Abdelhakim (Roam Morocco Co-Founders) Reading Time: 9 Minutes (2,100 Words) Focus: Morocco Film Location Tourism
Quick Answer: Where Was Gladiator II Filmed in Morocco?

Ridley Scott chose the fortified desert city of Ouarzazate and the UNESCO World Heritage site of Kasbah Ait Benhaddou in southern Morocco as the primary filming locations for Gladiator II. Production constructed custom Roman arena sets on the outdoor backlots of Atlas Film Studios, returning to the very same red-earthen valley where the original Oscar-winning Gladiator was filmed in 1999.

When director Ridley Scott envisioned the dusty, sun-bleached Roman gladiator training arena for the original 2000 film Gladiator, he did not build in Rome or Los Angeles. He journeyed to the desert foothills of southern Morocco, where the ancient mud-brick citadel of Ait Benhaddou rises majestically from the Ounila River valley.

Nearly a quarter-century later, Scott returned to the same storied valley to shoot Gladiator II. For movie lovers, walking through the narrow alleyways of Ait Benhaddou and exploring the colossal soundstages of Ouarzazate is one of the most unforgettable cultural excursions in North Africa.

How to Explore Kasbah Ait Benhaddou

Ait Benhaddou is a striking example of traditional pre-Saharan earthen clay architecture. Dating back to the 11th century, it was a pivotal stop along the trans-Saharan caravan route that transported gold, salt, and spices between Timbuktu and Marrakech.

LOCAL EXPERT ADVICE // BEATING THE TOUR BUS CROWDS

Massive tour buses from Marrakech arrive at Ait Benhaddou between 11:30 AM and 2:00 PM. To experience the kasbah in cinematic serenity, visit either early in the morning (08:30 AM) or late in the afternoon (after 04:30 PM) when the afternoon sun casts deep golden-amber hues across the rammed-earth walls.

Key Highlights Inside the Citadel

  • The Ounila Stepping Stones: Cross the shallow riverbed on natural stepping stones, retracing the steps where Hollywood film crews laid temporary Roman arenas.
  • The Upper Granary (Agadir): Hike the winding 15-minute path to the peak of the hill. From this panoramic vantage point, you look down over the entire date palm oasis and the snow-capped High Atlas Mountains in the background.
  • Saffron & Indigo Artisans: Inside the maze of alleys, local Berber painters create remarkable desert art using saffron, indigo, and tea, heating the paper over a small butane torch to reveal warm earthen colors.

Visiting Atlas Film Studios: The Desert Soundstage

Located just 5 kilometers outside Ouarzazate along the road to Marrakech, Atlas Film Studios covers more than 300,000 square meters of arid desert plateau. Founded in 1983 by Moroccan entrepreneur Mohamed Belghmi, it is one of the largest movie studios in the world by surface area.

What You Can Walk Through Today

  • The Egyptian Temple: Walk through towering pharaonic statues, stone sphinxes, and massive hieroglyphic columns constructed for productions like Asterix & Obelix: Mission Cleopatra.
  • The Roman Colosseum & Chariot Track: Examine the plaster, timber, and styrofoam sets engineered to look like weathered travertine stone in Gladiator and Gladiator II.
  • Tibetan Monasteries: Tour the mountain temple constructed for Martin Scorsese’s Kundun (Scorsese used Morocco to recreate Lhasa, Tibet).

Frequently Asked Questions: Morocco Film Location Tours

How much does it cost to enter Ait Benhaddou?

Entrance to Kasbah Ait Benhaddou itself is free, though certain restored private family residences within the ksar charge a modest fee of 10 to 20 MAD (approx. $1–$2 USD) to view their traditional living quarters. Hiring an official licensed local guide costs between 150 and 200 MAD ($15–$20 USD) for an enriching 90-minute historical tour.

What is the ticket price for Atlas Film Studios?

Standard general admission to Atlas Film Studios is 80 MAD (approx. $8 USD) per adult. A guided studio backlot tour is included with your ticket, led by studio staff who explain which scenes were shot on each soundstage.

Are filming sets permanent or torn down after movies finish?

At UNESCO Kasbah Ait Benhaddou, strict conservation regulations require production companies to dismantle temporary structures to preserve the historic mud-brick walls. However, at Atlas Film Studios and CLA Studios in Ouarzazate, major architectural sets (such as the Egyptian temples and medieval ramparts) are maintained permanently for tourists and future film productions.
