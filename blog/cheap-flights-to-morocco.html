By Roam Morocco Flight Desk • Updated for 2026 Seasons • 7 min read

Morocco is one of the most accessible exotic destinations in the world. Located just under three hours from London or Paris, and with direct non-stop flights from New York, Montreal, and major European hubs, getting here is surprisingly affordable—if you know how to search.

1. Marrakech (RAK) vs. Casablanca (CMN): Where Should You Land?

The two main gateways into Morocco serve very different traveler needs:

  • Marrakech Menara Airport (RAK): Best for leisure travelers heading directly into the red medina, Agafay Desert, or starting a Sahara Desert expedition. RAK is heavily served by European low-cost carriers (Ryanair, EasyJet, Transavia, Wizz Air).
  • Casablanca Mohammed V Airport (CMN): The primary international hub for intercontinental flights. If you are flying direct from New York (JFK), Washington (IAD), Montreal (YUL), or Doha/Dubai, you will likely land in Casablanca on Royal Air Maroc, Delta, or Emirates.
  • Fes–Saïss Airport (FEZ): A hidden gem for budget travelers. European flights to Fes are often 30–40% cheaper than Marrakech during peak months, and Fes is the perfect starting point for our north-to-south desert circuit.

2. Top Airlines for Budget Flights to Morocco

Depending on where you originate, these are the airlines offering the strongest pricing power:

  • From Europe: Ryanair, EasyJet, Transavia, Vueling, and TAP Air Portugal (connecting via Lisbon). Fares from London, Madrid, Marseille, and Milan can be as low as €25–€60 each way.
  • From North America: Royal Air Maroc operates daily direct non-stop flights from JFK and Montreal to Casablanca. Alternatively, TAP Air Portugal (via Lisbon) or Iberia (via Madrid) frequently offer round-trip transatlantic fares under $650.
  • From Australia / Asia: Qatar Airways (via Doha) and Emirates (via Dubai) offer seamless one-stop connections into Casablanca.

3. The Best Months for Cheap Morocco Flights

Flight prices to Morocco follow distinct seasonal waves:

  • Cheapest Months (Low Season): Mid-January through February, and June through early July. Fares drop significantly.
  • The Sweet Spot (Shoulder Season): Late October, November, and March offer pleasant desert temperatures and moderate flight fares.
  • Peak Expensive Weeks: Christmas & New Year's week, Easter holidays, and mid-April through May. If traveling during these periods, book at least 90–120 days in advance.
