Combining High-Speed Train & Private Driver in Morocco (The Smart Circuit)

How savvy travelers save time, cut costs, and avoid car fatigue by riding the 320 km/h Al Boraq bullet train in the north, then switching to a private 4x4 chauffeur for the Sahara.

Published: October 3, 2026 Author: Hamza & Abdelhakim (Roam Morocco Co-Founders) Reading Time: 10 Minutes (2,200 Words) Focus: Multi-Modal Morocco Itinerary Planning
Quick Answer: Can You Combine Trains and a Private Driver in Morocco?

Yes. The most efficient Morocco itinerary uses the Al Boraq high-speed bullet train (320 km/h) to zip effortlessly between northern urban centers (Tangier, Rabat, Casablanca) in under 2 hours, and express rail into Fes. In Fes, travelers meet their private 4x4 chauffeur to explore the rail-less southern half of the Kingdom: the Middle Atlas, Erg Chebbi sand dunes, Dades Gorge, and the High Atlas Mountains into Marrakech.

When travelers begin planning a dream vacation to Morocco, they often fall into one of two extremes: either attempting to travel everywhere exclusively by public transit (and discovering trains do not go anywhere near the Sahara dunes), or hiring a private chauffeur for two continuous weeks and spending hours sitting in highway traffic between northern commercial ports.

There is a much smarter, elegant solution that luxury travelers and seasoned planners use: The Multi-Modal Hybrid Circuit.

By pairing Africa’s fastest, sleekest bullet train—the French-engineered Al Boraq TGV—with a dedicated private 4x4 chauffeur for the rugged interior, you get the absolute best of both worlds: European-standard high-speed rail speed where it shines, and bespoke chauffeur flexibility where you truly need it.

The Ideal 8 to 10-Day Hybrid Blueprint (Train + Chauffeur)

Here is the exact step-by-step circuit curated by Roam Morocco for maximum comfort, scenic variety, and zero wasted travel hours:

Leg 1 (Days 1–3): The Northern Imperial Rail Arc

Route: Casablanca → Rabat → Tangier (or reverse)

  • Day 1: Arrive at Casablanca Mohammed V Airport (CMN). Tour the magnificent Hassan II Mosque overlooking the Atlantic Ocean.
  • Day 2: Board the high-speed Al Boraq train at Casa-Voyageurs station. Sip an espresso in First Class while reaching 320 km/h. Arrive in Rabat (45 min) to tour the Kasbah of the Udayas, or continue straight to Tangier (2h 10m).
  • Day 3: Take the scenic express train from Rabat or Tangier directly to the medieval imperial capital of Fes. Check into a boutique riad in Fes el-Bali.

Leg 2 (Days 4–7): The Private 4x4 Sahara Expedition

Route: Fes → Midelt → Merzouga Dunes → Dades Valley → Marrakech

  • Day 4: Meet Your Private Chauffeur: Your dedicated, English-speaking driver greets you directly at your riad gate or Fes Railway Station with a luxury, air-conditioned Toyota Land Cruiser Prado 4x4. Journey through the cedar forests of Azrou to feed wild Barbary macaques, traverse the dramatic Ziz Valley canyon, and arrive at the edge of the Erg Chebbi dunes in Merzouga for a luxury desert glamping overnight.
  • Day 5: Deep Sahara & Desert Culture: Explore nomad settlements, listen to spiritual Gnawa musicians in Khamlia, and take an exhilarating 4x4 dune drive before a second tranquil night under millions of Sahara stars.
  • Day 6: Canyons & Thousand Kasbahs: Travel through the towering 300m rock walls of Todra Gorge, the fragrant Valley of Roses, and into the lush Skoura palm oasis. Overnight at an authentic mud-brick heritage kasbah.
  • Day 7: Across the High Atlas into Marrakech: Tour the UNESCO World Heritage Ksar of Ait Benhaddou, cross the breathtaking 2,260m Tizi n'Tichka mountain pass, and arrive directly at your luxury riad in Marrakech by late afternoon.

Train Travel in Morocco: What You Need to Know

PRO TIP // FIRST CLASS ONCF TRAIN TICKETS

Always book First Class (Première Classe) on Moroccan trains. First Class tickets on Al Boraq cost only about $15 to $35 USD more than second class, but guarantee assigned spacious leather seating, silent climate-controlled carriages, dedicated luggage racks, power outlets, and a tranquil atmosphere.

How to Book Moroccan Train Tickets

Morocco’s national rail network is operated by ONCF. While you can purchase tickets at station kiosks, Al Boraq high-speed seats frequently sell out during holiday weekends and peak travel seasons (spring and autumn). You can book online directly via the official ONCF website (oncf-voyages.ma) up to 90 days in advance using international credit cards.

Luggage on Moroccan Trains

Unlike European airlines with strict baggage weight limits, Moroccan trains are generous. Each passenger is permitted two large suitcases plus a carry-on bag. First Class carriages feature luggage shelving at the entrance of each car and overhead compartments above your seat.

Is a Hybrid Circuit Cheaper than Full-Time Driver Hire?

Hiring a private luxury chauffeur for an entire 10-day trip typically costs $1,800 to $2,800 USD total (inclusive of vehicle, fuel, driver expenses, and highway tolls). By using the high-speed train for the northern leg (Casablanca/Tangier to Fes) and hiring the chauffeur for the 5-day southern desert and mountain stretch (Fes to Marrakech), travelers can save $400 to $800 USD while actually traveling faster between northern cities.

Frequently Asked Questions: Train + Driver in Morocco

Can you take a train from Marrakech to the Sahara Desert?

No. The Moroccan rail network terminates in Marrakech in the southwest and Oujda/Nador in the northeast. There are no railway lines crossing the High Atlas Mountains or extending into Merzouga or Zagora. The desert is accessible exclusively by road via private 4x4 vehicle or bus.

Are trains in Morocco clean and safe for tourists?

Yes, extremely. The Al Boraq high-speed train is on par with France’s TGV or Spain’s AVE, featuring immaculate restrooms, dedicated onboard conductors, and security staff at every station.

How will my private driver find me at Fes Railway Station?

When booking with Roam Morocco, your chauffeur tracks your train’s arrival time in real time and waits directly outside the main station concourse holding a personalized welcome sign. They assist with your heavy luggage and guide you directly to your awaiting air-conditioned 4x4.
