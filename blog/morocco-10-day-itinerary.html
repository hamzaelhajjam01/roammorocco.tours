The Ultimate 10-Day Morocco Itinerary: Imperial Cities, Atlas & Sahara

The definitive route connecting Casablanca, Fes, Merzouga's golden Erg Chebbi dunes, and Marrakech with realistic drive times and private driver recommendations.

Published: September 28, 2026 Author: Hamza & Abdelhakim (Roam Morocco Co-Founders) Reading Time: 14 Minutes (2,900 Words) Focus: Imperial Cities & Sahara Route

So, you have booked your flights and are wondering: "What is a good itinerary for a 10-day trip in Morocco?" Whether you are searching for a Morocco road trip 10 days route, or the classic 10 day itinerary from Marrakech to Fes, you have landed in exactly the right place.

Morocco is a country of staggering contrasts. In just ten days, you can wander the car-free, medieval medina of Fes, cross the snow-capped High Atlas Mountains, and ride a camel into the shifting sand dunes of the Sahara Desert. This guide outlines our absolute favorite Morocco 10-day itinerary.

Days 1-2: Arriving in Marrakech & Jemaa el Fna

Your 10 days in Morocco begin in the vibrant "Red City." Marrakech is an assault on the senses in the best possible way. Spend your first day getting lost in the winding, maze-like alleys of the old medina.

  • Jemaa el Fna: As evening approaches, head to the world-famous Jemaa el Fnaa square. The air fills with the smoke of food stalls, the sounds of snake charmers, and the calls of merchants.
  • Royal Palaces & Tombs: Dedicate your second day to history. Visit the intricately carved Bahia Palace, the peaceful Menara Gardens, and the ancient Saadian Tombs. End your day sipping mint tea overlooking the imposing Koutoubia Mosque.

Curator's Tip: When plunging into the souks for the first time, keep your pace unhurried. If you feel anxious about navigating or haggling, read our insider cultural guide on The Art of the Souk: Why Bargaining in Marrakech is a Conversation, Not Combat, and consult our seasonal advice on what to wear in Morocco so you stay cool, comfortable, and culturally respectful.

Day 3: The High Atlas Mountains & Tizi n'Tichka Pass

Leave the chaotic energy of the city behind and begin your scenic route into the High Atlas Mountains. The drive via the sweeping Tizi n Tichka pass (reaching 2,260m) is one of the most stunning mountain roads in Africa. Stop frequently to take photos of the mud-brick Berber villages clinging to the steep canyon walls.

Curator's Logistics Note // Hamza & Abdelhakim

Many travelers ask us whether they should self-drive this mountain pass in a rental car. Between heavy commercial trucks navigating tight hairpins and sudden altitude shifts, it can test even experienced drivers. If you want to relax and watch the walnut groves glide past without white-knuckle stress, weigh the tradeoffs in our guide on renting a car in Morocco vs hiring a private driver, and review our transparent pricing breakdown on Morocco private driver costs.

Day 4: Ait Benhaddou & The Thousand Kasbahs

Descend from the mountains to explore a true UNESCO World Heritage site: the ancient Ksar of Ait Benhaddou. Travelers looking for dedicated movie set circuits can book our tailored Morocco film location & cinema tour. This ancient fortified village was a crucial stop on the former caravan route between the Sahara and Marrakech.

Film lovers will instantly recognize it. If you've ever watched Gladiator, Game of Thrones, or Lawrence of Arabia, you've seen this stunning mud-brick fortress. From here, continue the short drive to Ouarzazate to visit the massive Atlas Studios (the Hollywood of Africa) and the historic Taourirt Kasbah, once home to the powerful Glaoui family.

Day 5: Dades Gorge & Todra Gorge

As you drive along the "Route of 1000 Kasbahs", the landscape dramatically changes. You'll pass through the lush, palm-filled Draa Valley before arriving at the towering limestone river canyons of the Todra Gorge. These massive cliff walls reach up to 300 meters high. Spend the night near the Dades Gorge, where the winding roads ("monkey fingers" rock formations) offer incredible sunset views.

Days 6-7: The Sahara Desert & Erg Chebbi Sand Dunes

No Morocco itinerary (7, 10 & 14 days) is complete without the Sahara Desert. The drive to Merzouga across the palm-fringed Ziz Valley is entirely worth every mile.

Upon arriving at the edge of the Erg Chebbi sand dunes, leave the motorized world behind and embark on a traditional camel trek or private 4x4 transfer into the deep dunes. Watch the sunset turn the sands from gold to deep crimson. Spend the night at a luxury desert camp under a blanket of stars, eating traditional Berber cuisine and listening to drum rhythms around the tamarisk fire.

Planning Note: If you are debating between Morocco's different dune fields, read our comprehensive comparison on Merzouga vs Zagora Sahara tours. Make sure to check the best time to visit the Sahara to plan around summer heat or winter night freezes, or explore our handcrafted 3-Day Sahara Luxury Explorer tour.

Days 8-9: Arriving in Fes & The Medieval Medina

After an early start to watch the desert sunrise, begin the drive north toward the imperial city of Fes. The landscape shifts again, passing through the cedar forests of the Middle Atlas (keep an eye out for Barbary macaques).

Fes is the spiritual and cultural heart of Morocco. Its car-free, medieval medina (Fes el-Bali) is a labyrinth of over 9,000 narrow alleys. We highly recommend you hire an official local guide here, as Google Maps cannot navigate the covered passageways. Visit the famous Chouara tanneries in Fes, the stunning Bou Inania Madrasa, and shop for exquisite local leather goods and ceramics along the ancient medina paths.

Curator's Insight: To understand why Fes feels so profoundly different from the bustling energy of the south, explore our comparative guide on Marrakech vs Fes: Which Moroccan Imperial City Should You Visit?.

Day 10: Casablanca or Return to Marrakech

Depending on where your departure flight leaves from, Day 10 is your transit day. If you are flying out of Casablanca, a 10-day Morocco itinerary usually involves taking the fast train or your private transfer from Fes. If you pass through Casablanca, ensure you visit the monumental Hassan II Mosque, one of the few mosques in Morocco open to non-Muslims. If your flight is out of Marrakech, it's a straight highway drive to conclude your journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a great 4-5 day itinerary for first time visit to Morocco?

If you only have 4-5 days, focus solely on Marrakech and the immediate surroundings. You can spend 2 days in the city and take our 3-Day Premium Explorer to visit the Agafay Desert, Ourika Valley, and the Essaouira coast without wasting time on long bus rides.

Is 10 days in Morocco enough time?

Yes, 10 days is the "sweet spot" for a Moroccan vacation. It gives you enough time to comfortably visit the imperial cities (Marrakech and Fes), cross the High Atlas Mountains, and spend a night in the deep Sahara Desert without feeling utterly exhausted.
